How ETAX works — four steps from a new buyer to an invoice filed with FBR

Watch the 1-minute demo, or follow the steps below. Each step opens the page where you do it.

＋ Create an invoice
1

Add your buyer

  • Open Buyers / Customers.
  • Enter the NTN or CNIC, STRN, registered / unregistered, name, province and address.
  • Add mobile, email and a note if you like, then Save buyer.
  • Many buyers at once? Use Excel template → Import from Excel.
Go to Buyers / Customers →
2

Set up your items and tax rules

  • Open Items with Tax Rules → ＋ Add / edit item.
  • 1 Item details and 2 Prices (one or more, e.g. Retail and Wholesale).
  • 3 Tax rule: sale type, sales tax rate, references and a comment if you like (optional) and any more taxes.
  • 4 Review & save — every section shows a green ✓ when it is complete.
Go to Items with Tax Rules →
3

Create a new invoice

  • Open ＋ New Invoice.
  • Buyer: search a saved buyer, + Add new customer, or Walk-in customer.
  • Items: pick the item, enter the quantity and tick the price — the tax is calculated automatically.
  • Under each item you see exactly what is sent to FBR.
Go to New Invoice →
4

Save as draft, or submit to FBR

  • Save as draft to finish later (Draft Invoices), or Submit to FBR.
  • FBR returns its invoice number; print the A4 invoice or the 80 mm receipt with the QR code.
  • The invoice is now Issued (Locked) — corrections go through a debit note.
  • Find every filed invoice under Issued Invoices.
Go to Issued Invoices →
