Put an authority's official logo here to have it shown across the app, named by the authority's short name in lower case:
fbr.png  pra.png  srb.png  kpra.png  bra.png  (and psw.png, secp.png, eobi.png, essi.png when those are used)
Use the logo as the authority publishes it: a PNG, about 200 pixels tall, a clear or white background. Until a file is here the app shows the
authority's name in a plain badge, so nothing breaks. Do not edit, recolour or redraw a logo. See docs/AUTHORITY_LOGOS.md.
