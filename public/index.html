Questions

Do I need anything from FBR first?+

You need an active NTN/STRN and an IRIS login. In IRIS you select Digital Invoicing with PRAL as the integrator; we help you fill it in and whitelist the server.

What happens if the internet or FBR is down?+

Your invoice is saved and queued. Raseed retries automatically and files it as soon as FBR responds — and it never files the same sale twice.

Can I upload invoices from Excel?+

Yes. Download our template, fill one row per item, and upload. You see every error before anything is sent, and re-uploading the same file never creates duplicates.

Can my customers verify the invoice?+

Every receipt shows the FBR invoice number and a QR code with the FBR Digital Invoicing logo, which customers can check in FBR's Tax Asaan app.

Is my data safe?+

Each business only sees its own data. Your FBR token is stored encrypted, and every action is recorded in a log that cannot be edited or deleted.

How much does it cost?+

It depends on your business and invoice volume. Contact us for a free demo and a quote.
