Questions

Do I need anything from FBR first?+

You need an active NTN/STRN and an IRIS login. We help you switch on Digital Invoicing in IRIS and do the technical FBR connection for you.

What happens if the internet or FBR is down?+

Your invoice is saved and queued. ETAX retries automatically and files it as soon as FBR responds — and it never files the same sale twice.

Can I upload invoices from Excel?+

Yes. Download our template, fill one row per item, and upload. You see every error before anything is sent, and re-uploading the same file never creates duplicates.

Can my customers verify the invoice?+

Every receipt shows the FBR invoice number and a QR code with the FBR Digital Invoicing logo, which customers can check in FBR's Tax Asaan app.

Is my data safe?+

Each business only sees its own data. Your FBR connection key is stored encrypted, and every action is recorded in a log that cannot be edited or deleted.

How much does it cost?+

We sell annual plans only: Starter Rs. 12,000 a year, Pro Rs. 30,000 a year and Enterprise Rs. 90,000 a year. Those are 50% off the full prices (Rs. 24,000, Rs. 60,000 and Rs. 180,000). See Pricing for what each plan includes, or contact us for a free demo.

Do you also support PRA (Punjab)?+

Yes. ETAX also has a Point of Sale that sends every sale to the Punjab Revenue Authority's eIMS, for restaurants, banquet halls, hotels and other Punjab service businesses. It costs the same as FBR Digital Invoicing, and a business can use both with one login.

What happens when my year ends?+

Your plan runs for a year from the day we set you up. After that there is a short grace period (3 days on a monthly plan, 7 days on a yearly one), and then the account is paused until you renew. Nothing is deleted.
