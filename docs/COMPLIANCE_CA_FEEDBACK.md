## Questions for the CA

1. **How often it comes back (3).** A three-month period comes back every quarter, one month every month; a quarter that is one
   of four separate returns can be set "once a year". Is that right?
2. **Ticking a client (10).** The tick boxes are also used for actions on several rows (next period, step, assign, archive), so
   ticking shows **Only this client** rather than filtering at once. Is one extra click acceptable?
3. **Which year (5, 10).** A period belongs to the year of its **due date**: the income tax return for 1 Jul 2026 – 30 Jun 2027,
   due 30 Sep 2027, shows under 2027. Or should it show in every year its period touches?
4. **Cancelling a paid invoice (17).** Its receipts are voided with it. Is that right, or should the money stay as a credit or refund?
5. **Header (13).** It is a text line plus the logo. Is a full-width letterhead picture wanted instead?
6. **No WhatsApp (4).** Should such reminders be markable as "reminded by phone / email" so they leave the list?
