## Questions for the CA

1. **How often it comes back (3).** A three-month period comes back every quarter, one month every month; a quarter that is one
   of four separate returns can be set "once a year". Is that right?
2. **Ticking a client (10).** The tick boxes are also used for actions on several rows (next period, step, assign, archive), so
   ticking shows **Only this client** rather than filtering at once. Is one extra click acceptable?
3. **Which year (5, 10).** A period belongs to the year of its **due date**: the income tax return for 1 Jul 2026 – 30 Jun 2027,
   due 30 Sep 2027, shows under 2027. Or should it show in every year its period touches?
4. **Cancelling a paid invoice (17).** Its receipts are voided with it. Is that right, or should the money stay as a credit or refund?
5. **Header (13).** It is a text line plus the logo. Is a full-width letterhead picture wanted instead?
6. **No WhatsApp (4).** Should such reminders be markable as "reminded by phone / email" so they leave the list?

## Questions for the CA (round 3)

1. **Overall status (7).** The actions counted are: each reminder (one no longer needed because the task was done first is not
   counted), data collection, task, invoice created, invoice sent and receipt (money received in full): 8 with three reminders.
   The advance payment is not counted (not every job has one). Is that the count you want?
2. **Invoices Created / Issued (12).** "Created" = made but not yet given to the client; "Issued" = sent to the client (on WhatsApp
   or marked sent) or paid. Is that the split you meant?
3. **Receipt column (7).** It shows Pending once the invoice is sent; "+ Receipt" records the money, then its WhatsApp button sends
   the receipt. Is that the order you meant by "after that an option here to mark as receipt"?
4. **Advance receipts (13).** An advance is recorded with its date, description and amount, without a receipt number of its own. Should
   an advance also get a numbered receipt for the client?
5. **"Used for" (5).** Removed from the obligation window. Should it also go from Setup → Message Templates (where it decides which
   template is offered first for an invoice or a receipt message)?
